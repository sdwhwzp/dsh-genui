# Saved fence aliases and DOM diagnostics

A correctly labelled dsh-ui fence can fail semantic validation. Both rendering channels must preserve source and identify the rejected field. The DOM channel owns diagnostic siblings separately from React mounts, removes them after repair or disposal, and never nests a fallback CodeBlock that its observer would discover again.

The hero tone brand maps deterministically to accent before validation; unrelated invalid tones remain errors. Persisted Session messages are never rewritten to repair presentation.
